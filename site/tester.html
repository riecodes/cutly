Be a tester.

Free, open source, and yours first.

Google Play asks for twelve testers before a new app from a personal account can launch. Cutly needs those twelve, and they get it before anyone else.

Android 10 and up. Cutly is free: nothing to pay now or later.

  1. Leave your Gmail below. Play invites testers by Google account, so it has to be a Gmail one.
  2. The Play invite comes to that Gmail. Accept it, then install Cutly from the Play Store like any other app.
  3. Keep it on your phone for fourteen days. That is what Play counts.
  4. Shoot a take or two, and tell me what breaks, what is confusing, and what you would throw away.

Your Gmail and phone model are used only to run the test. See the privacy policy.
